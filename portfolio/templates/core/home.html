About

I care about the scientific questions first — then the right tool.

My research has ranged from experimental evolution of Saccharomyces cerevisiae in the Heil Lab at NC State — where I built a full GATK best-practices variant calling pipeline and contributed bioinformatic analyses to a conference-submitted manuscript — to computer vision for marine biology in the Dornburg Lab at UNC Charlotte, where I'm training YOLOv8 to classify surgeonfish from underwater imagery.

Alongside academic work, I build things for myself. Court IQ is a generative-AI tennis coach that takes point-by-point match data and returns specific strategies, drills, and conditioning plans. Projects like that keep me honest about the difference between a cool prototype and software that does real work.

I'm looking for roles in the biotechnology industry where I can apply computational and machine-learning approaches to genuinely interesting biology — drug discovery, diagnostics, genomics, protein engineering, or wherever sequence and image data need to become insight.
